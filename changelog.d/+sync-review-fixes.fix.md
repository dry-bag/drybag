The note sync no longer lets a note's name or its opening bytes exempt a secret from its scan: every scan reads a patch of the bytes under neutral names, in a repository of its own, so neither the default rules' path allowlist nor gitleaks' own skipping of what looks binary applies. The session's environment no longer chooses where the job's notes go or which scanner runs (`GIT_CONFIG_GLOBAL`, `url.*.insteadOf`, `GIT_SSH_COMMAND`, `PRE_COMMIT_HOME` and the like reach none of its git), and the job runs none of the overlay's git hooks. A rewrite of notes already pushed, by a reset or a rebase, is held, not undone: the job names the commits and `stayfixed doctor` gives the force-push and the rebase. `doctor` also says when the sync is not running (a job that `skipped` is no longer green, and a failure a rerun meets again says so) and names a stale `index.lock`, which the job's own time limit no longer leaves behind. A Codex session starts nothing. An unreachable ssh host now ends as `timeout` after the step's 30 seconds, where it used to end as `offline` after 10.
