`stayfixed init` is gone, with no alias, and so are its `init` skill and the shipped project templates. Write a `stayfixed.toml` by hand: `[stayfixed] version` and `[project] name` are enough. `docs trail` keeps working over a `docs/roadmap.md` and a `trail.toml` you write yourself, and `docs/cli.md` gives their shape; without them it has nothing to check. The preset's `[plugins] recommended` list is gone too, since only `init` read it, and a `stayfixed:ignore` region an earlier `init` left in `.gitignore` is yours to keep or remove. A command that finds no `stayfixed.toml` now says to write one.
